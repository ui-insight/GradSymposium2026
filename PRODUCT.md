# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Conference attendees find their poster number on phones while setting up in the
ballroom. Judges score presentations, and organizers manage the roster and results.

## Product Purpose

Give each accepted presentation a stable identity and help attendees find its
number and location quickly. Support rubric judging and attendee voting through
distinct workflows against the same roster.

## Brand Personality

Clear, welcoming, practical. Retain the existing University of Idaho gold/ink
palette and familiar typography, as confirmed by Barrie on October 27, 2026.

## Anti-references

Keep poster lookup a simple searchable list. Avoid decorative dashboards or
marketing layouts that distract from finding a poster number.

## Design Principles

- Make the poster number immediately visible.
- Preserve full titles and presenter names, including co-presenters.
- Give organizers control over when draft roster information becomes public.
- Keep public lookup accessible without a login.
- Reuse familiar interface patterns and the existing visual style.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a light, accessible phone layout, as confirmed by Barrie. Provide explicit
labels, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, large touch targets, and clear
loading, empty, and error states. Do not require motion to understand information.
